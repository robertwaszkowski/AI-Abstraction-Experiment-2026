# Acceptance and development endpoints

The 14 preserved author reports document checks against the prescribed scenarios and acceptance determinations. The investigator confirms that the reports were completed during evaluation and that no required Code-team developer repairs occurred after the recorded development endpoints. This confirmation is recorded separately from the original reports. Exact evaluation dates are not stated in those reports; no dates have been reconstructed.

`run_by_scenario_matrix.csv` and `reported_checklist.csv` transcribe the report checklists. `endpoint_evidence.csv` connects the time-record types and source locators with the available completion evidence. Run the root `build.py` to regenerate these tables. The original requirements are in `../task_inputs/`; report hashes are in `report_manifest.json`.

Cross-team peer evaluation, the investigator's author check, and capsule structural validation are distinct. The reports document the author check; they do not themselves supply the confidential peer-assignment table. That table remains in private study records and is not included here. Persona impersonation and role-specific queues do not establish independently tested resistance to unauthorized access. Acceptance does not establish equal residual defect density or exhaustive correctness.

The portable capsule transcribes and checks these records. It does not re-execute participant applications, authenticate the evaluator's identity, independently recode observations, or test deployed security. Raw histories and participant-level assignment records remain restricted.
