# Effective team requirements

The workflow application requirements are consolidated in [the team instructions](team_instructions_corrected.txt). The same requirements apply to both development approaches.

Each two-person team implements all three supplied business processes using both AI-assisted application-code generation and AI-assisted generation of models for Aurea:

- Leave Request;
- Change of Employment Conditions;
- Decorations and Medals.

The applications must implement the process logic, data, and role responsibilities defined by the supplied BPMN models, data descriptions, and role mapping. Evaluation covers positive and negative process-model branches. Teams continue development until the application requirements are achieved and accepted.

**Process-diagram preview.** The application requirements include access to a diagram of the business process.

Reusable starting applications are permitted, including an existing Role Call application supplying login functionality. Standalone Role Call development is outside the three-workflow study; adapting a starter to implement those workflows is relevant study work.

Teams may choose their AI tools and models. Both members work together, including inspecting generated results, discussing changes, checking requirements, and correcting the applications. Teams record their work and preserve AI prompts with dates and times.

Implementation, inspection and correction of the required application features, including process-diagram preview, form part of application development.

The supplementary task inputs provide the process definitions, data descriptions, test scenarios and role mappings for the three workflows.
