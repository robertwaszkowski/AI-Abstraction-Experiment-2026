# Descriptive scope

Profiles describe distinct IT category names involved in recorded human and AI activities or supported by the chosen application implementation. They do not measure individual mastery, minimum human skills, difficulty or intensity. The same taxonomy and scope rules apply to both strategies and all actors.

The 329 source-linked assignments comprise 163 observed-use/report, 125 implementation-supported and 41 excluded rows, with zero unresolved assignments. Positive O/R evidence is deduplicated by exact category name after IT-ID filtering. Unresolved assignments alone do not add a category. Common-task and starter-adaptation evidence is included; additional preview and outside Role Call remain separately labelled. No independent second assessment was conducted. Source coverage is partial; absence of an annotation does not establish absence of the activity.
